Group Project Presentation

Students must prepare a comprehensive group presentation that includes:

• An introduction to their chosen topic.

• The methodology employed - AI Methods/Tools/ Techniques

• Results obtained.

• A discussion on the implications of their work.

• Ethical considerations and potential societal impact of their project.

Implementation

• Develop their AI project using suitable tools, algorithms, and datasets.

• Utilize existing AI frameworks or libraries, or pseudo coding. It is encouraged to build a working system and visualization panel for the showcase. 

• Document their development process, highlighting challenges faced and solutions implemented.


Grading will be assessed based on:

1. Problem framing and Relevance

2. AI Integration and Understanding

3. Methodology/ Tool Evaluation

4. Interdisciplinary and Ethical Insight
